---
serves: 6
time: 1 hour
tags: [recipe, soup]
---
1. Roast two squash, halved, at 200 °C for 40 minutes.
2. Soften an onion and two cloves of garlic in butter.
3. Add the squash, a litre of stock and a pinch of nutmeg.
4. Blend until smooth. Finish with cream and black pepper.

> [!note]
> It freezes well. Make a double batch for the [[Cabin Weekend]].
