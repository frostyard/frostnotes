# T-0047: Draw menus with items, separators and submenus

## Goal

Every menu in the window, a tab's context menu, the tab list, More
options, the vault menu, the Files pane's sort order and the status
bar's editing mode, is one component (`docs/specs/ui.md`, Menus). Draw
it as a presentational component: a list of items at a point, moved by
the keyboard and the pointer, with checkable items and submenus. The
containers decide what each item does.

## Contract

Keep every exported name and signature; replace the stub and the file's
"Task T-0047 …" sentence with a description of the menu.

**Where:** `Menu` renders through `createPortal` into `document.body`,
so no ancestor clips it. The list is a `div` with `role="menu"`,
`aria-label={label}`, `tabIndex={-1}`, `position: fixed` (the `fixed`
class) with `style.left = x` and `style.top = y` in pixels. After it
lays out (`useLayoutEffect`), move it left or up so it stays 8px inside
the window (`window.innerWidth`, `window.innerHeight`, its
`getBoundingClientRect()`), never past 8px from the left or top. On
mount it takes focus (the list element itself).

**Look:** `bg-window`, 1px `border-separator`, rounded 8 (`rounded-lg`),
`shadow-popover`, 4px padding, at least 180 wide, `z-50`. Items 28 high
(`h-7`), `text-sidebar-row`, 8px side padding, rounded 4, 8px gap:
first a 16-wide column holding the item's `icon` (or, for a checkable
item, a 14px Lucide `Check` when checked and nothing when not), in
`text-secondary`; then the label, truncated; then `keys`, if any, 12/16
in `text-tertiary` with 16px left margin; a submenu ends with a 14px
Lucide `ChevronRight`. The highlighted item is `bg-accent
text-accent-contrast`, and its icon, keys and chevron take that color
too (drop `text-secondary`/`text-tertiary` while highlighted). A
disabled item has `opacity-40`. A separator is an `<hr>` with 4px
vertical margins, 1px high, `bg-separator`, no border.

**Items**, in `items`' order, each a direct child of the menu element:

- `kind: "item"`: a `div` with `role="menuitem"`, or
  `role="menuitemcheckbox"` with `aria-checked` when `checked` is
  defined; `aria-label={label}` (so `keys` stay out of its name);
  `aria-disabled="true"` when disabled; `tabIndex={-1}`.
- `kind: "submenu"`: a `div` with `role="menuitem"`, `aria-label`,
  `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded` (`"true"` while its submenu is
  open, else `"false"`), `tabIndex={-1}`.
- `kind: "separator"`: the `<hr>`.

**Highlight:** none at first. ArrowDown moves it to the next enabled
item (an item that is not disabled, or a submenu), ArrowUp to the
previous, skipping separators and disabled items and wrapping at both
ends; with nothing highlighted, ArrowDown starts at the first enabled
item and ArrowUp at the last. The highlighted item takes focus. The
pointer entering an enabled item (`onPointerEnter`) highlights it (and
focuses it); entering a disabled one changes nothing.

**Choosing:** a click on an enabled item, or Enter or Space on the
highlighted one, calls `onChoose(id)` and then `onClose()`. A click on a
disabled item does nothing.

**Keys** on the menu (`onKeyDown` on the list; key events from the
focused item bubble to it): handle ArrowDown, ArrowUp, Enter, Space,
ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, Escape and Tab, calling `preventDefault()` on
each; call `stopPropagation()` on every key event, so the window's
keymap never sees a key pressed in a menu. Escape and Tab call
`onClose()` (Escape in a submenu: below).

**Closing:** a `pointerdown` anywhere outside the menu and its open
submenus (listen on `document`, in the capture phase) calls
`onClose()`; one inside does not. The menu prevents the default of
`contextmenu` events in it (the webview's own menu never shows).

**Submenus:** a submenu item opens its submenu when highlighted by the
pointer, when clicked, or with ArrowRight (or Enter or Space) while it
is highlighted. The submenu is another menu list in the same portal,
`role="menu"` with `aria-label` the submenu's label, its top left at the
item's right edge and 4px above the item's top (the item's
`getBoundingClientRect()`), and it takes focus. In it the keys work as
above, except: ArrowLeft and Escape close only the submenu, highlight
its item in the parent and focus that item; choosing an item calls the
menu's `onChoose(id)` and `onClose()`. While a submenu is open, the
parent ignores keys. The pointer entering another item of the parent
closes the open submenu. Submenus may nest.

## Gotchas

- Biome cannot read a role chosen by an expression (`role={checkable ?
  "menuitemcheckbox" : "menuitem"}`): it then reports
  `useAriaPropsSupportedByRole`, `noStaticElementInteractions` and
  `useKeyWithClickEvents` on the item. A `biome-ignore` comment naming
  those three rules with that reason is acceptable on that element only.
  Fix every other finding.
- A separator in a `div` with `role="separator"` fails Biome's
  `useSemanticElements`; use `<hr>`.
- happy-dom lays nothing out: `getBoundingClientRect()` is all zeros
  there, so the clamp must leave `x` and `y` alone when the menu fits.
- Key events from a focused item bubble to the list; a submenu is a
  sibling in the portal, not a child of its parent's list, so its keys
  do not reach the parent.
- The menu is presentational: no stores, no client, no workspace
  imports (`docs/tasks/EXECUTOR.md`).
